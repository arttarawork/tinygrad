## 2. What the 09-15 → 09-17 sprint established (details: HANDOFF_2026-09-13 §5d/§5e)

- **Small models beside the 27B.** Benchmarked on a fixed 20-instance SWE-bench Verified subset (`~/Documents/tinygrad-bench-data/swebench-small/`, mini-swe-agent + the official harness, x86_64 images emulated on colima): **Ornith-1.0-9B 12/20 resolved** (every submitted patch passed; misses = 32k overflows), K2-Horizon-3.7B 5/20 greedy → 3/20 with its card's settings, Ling 1–2/20, Maple 0/20 (twice), Nemotron 4B 4 patches. **Ornith-1.5-9B** (70.6 published; same speed as 1.0) is the worker; **Q5_K_M** by our own KL test (96.1 % top-1 vs Q8_0; Q4_K_M 93.4 %). Rule learned three times: benchmark thinking models with their card's sampling, never greedy.
- **Speculative decoding is a net loss on Metal** (0.8B/2B drafts and self-MTP all slower than 21 tok/s despite 65–70 % acceptance). Not a lever here.
- **T4.113 tensor-core prefill attention** (`NV_ATTN_WMMA=1`): +31 % prefill at 61k, +14 % at 20k on the card, argmax identical, flip gate 12/1152 near-tie; on the wire 131/128 tok/s. Lesson: a fully unrolled custom kernel can exceed the parallel-compile pool's pickling depth (surfaces as a repr RecursionError) — loop with RANGE.
- **T4.111 prefix snapshot** live (new sessions in ~2 s) + its empty-prefix bug fixed (requests without a system prompt crashed the connection). **T4.116** no tool-loop snapshots.
- **Memory/disk discipline**: VZ has no balloon (VM cap is the only lever → 6 GiB); the sparse colima disk never shrinks without `fstrim`; a disk-full event makes the VM read-only (`vm_recover.sh`); one small server at a time; delete superseded GGUFs (123 GB freed, the 35B fallback is gone).
- **Ops surface**: RUNBOOK.md, healthcheck.sh (busy-aware), daily-maintenance + Ornith LaunchAgents, `deploy_start.sh` generated from `deploy.sh`, `vm_recover.sh`, `hermes_update.sh` (prepared, not run).
